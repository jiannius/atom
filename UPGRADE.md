# Upgrading to atom 4

Each change that breaks a host is listed once, under the release that made it. The entries are written as the work lands; the codemod and the full checklist come with the 4.0 release.

## Installing a pre-release

Pin an exact version: `composer require jiannius/atom:4.0.0-alpha.1`. Composer accepts an exact pre-release version even under `"minimum-stability": "stable"`, so `composer.json` needs no other change. A range is different: `^4.0` alone never picks the alpha; `^4.0@alpha` does, or a lower `minimum-stability`.

## Tokens

**CSS integration.** Delete your atom `@source` lines and the theme block copied from skeleton-project (the font, `primary`, the zinc remap, `muted`, `accent`, the `border-color` base rule). Add `@import '../../vendor/jiannius/atom/resources/css/atom.tailwind.css';` after `@import 'tailwindcss';`. Then set your brand colour as `--color-accent` in `@theme`, and do not set `accent-foreground` unless you must. A derived colour you do set goes in `@theme` too, not in a plain `:root` rule: `accent-soft` and `accent-ink` are inline tokens, which utilities read where they are used. Overriding `--color-accent-ink` changes the `accent-ink` utilities only: `accent-content` and the focus ring read atom's `--atom-accent-ink` role directly (so a nested `.dark` island keeps its own values), and a `--color-accent-ink` override does not reach them.

**Root size.** The 14px root is gone; spacing utilities follow Tailwind (`p-6` is 24px, not 21px). Remove any `html { font-size }` override.

**Type sizes and density.** `text-xs`/`sm`/`base` follow the density profile of the page: compact (`layouts.sidebar`) renders them at 12/13/14px, comfortable (a bare `<atom:html>`, `layouts.auth`) at 13/14/15px. Pass `density="compact"` or `"comfortable"` to `<atom:html>` to change a page, or set `data-atom-density` on any element for a subtree. A touch device (`pointer: coarse`) gets taller controls and rows in either profile. `text-lg`, `text-xl` and `text-2xl` are now 16, 18 and 22px, and `font-medium` and `font-semibold` are weights 510 and 590 (Geist is a variable font, so they draw as asked).

**Browsers.** The derived accent colours (`accent-foreground`, `accent-hover`, `accent-ink`) and the focus ring use CSS relative colour: Chrome and Edge 119, Safari 16.4, Firefox 128. Below that floor atom falls back to plain colours: a white label on the accent, the accent itself for hover and for accent text, and a blue (`#3b82f6`) focus ring, so the focus ring stays visible and a dark accent keeps a readable label. A light accent does not: the label is always white, so on a light accent such as `#f5c518` it is about 1.6:1 and hard to read. Hover shows no change.

**Accent fallback.** If your app does not set `--color-accent`, the accent is near-black (`#1c1b18`), which is barely visible as a fill in dark mode. A dark-mode app should set its own accent.

**Font.** Geist is the default. Pass `fonts="inter"` to `<atom:html>` to keep the old font, or `:fonts="false"` to load none and use the system fonts.

**Remove the font you loaded for the old design.** atom serves Geist and Geist Mono itself: the files come from its own `/atom/{file}` route and `<atom:html>` (so every shell) preloads them. Delete the Vite font plugin (a Bunny or Google Fonts plugin, and its entry in `vite.config.js`) and any `@font-face` or `--font-sans` you wrote for the old font, or the page downloads a second font for nothing. Keep one only if you pass `fonts="inter"` (or another family) on purpose.

**Delete the copied `border-color` base rule.** Tailwind 4 draws a border in `currentColor`, so apps copied a base rule that gives every element a grey default border, in different shapes (for example `border-color: var(--color-gray-200, currentColor)` on `*, ::after, ::before`, alone or inside `@layer base`). Delete whatever rule does that, whatever its colour or selector list. atom now sets the default border colour itself, on `*`, `::after`, `::before`, `::backdrop` and `::file-selector-button`, from its `--atom-border` role (so it follows light and dark), in a layer so your own rule still wins. A copy left in place wins, and every border that has no colour utility of its own draws in the copied colour, not atom's.

## Shells

`layouts.header` is new: use it for apps with about 6 or fewer top-level sections. It takes the same slots and props as `layouts.sidebar`, including the same `nav` markup, so an app can choose the shell per user (for example, the top nav for a role that sees only one or two modules). `<main>` is still unpadded; padding now comes from `<atom:page>`.

**What changes in the shells you already use.** `layouts.sidebar` is restyled: a 228px side surface, 28px nav rows with 16px icons, and a 48px sticky top bar that is now shown at every width below `lg`, and from `lg` up only when it has something to hold (it holds the new `crumbs` and `actions` slots, and the dark toggle when `dark` is set, which used to show on small screens only; remove a toggle you put in `navfoot` yourself). Both shells also take new slots, `context` (a workspace switcher) and `search`. Navigation items now carry `aria-current="page"`, and a `navlist.group` heading is 12px and no longer uppercase. `layouts.auth` is restyled with the comfortable density and a plain canvas (the dark gradient is gone).

**A class on a headed `navlist.group`.** `<atom:navlist.group heading="..." class="...">` now puts its attributes on the group's root element (the heading, the items and the header shell's dropdown trigger), not on the inner list of items as before. A class you passed to space or style that list now applies to the whole group; move it onto the items or wrap them. A group with no `heading` is unchanged.

**The top nav switches to a menu below 1024px.** `layouts.header` shows its items as a bar from `lg` up (below that the logo, context, items and account do not fit side by side). Below `lg` the nav is a menu, with the `context` slot and then the `search` slot as the first two rows of the panel. The `search` slot is printed once and shown three ways: that full-width row below `lg`; from `lg` to `xl` (1280px) a search icon button in the bar opens it in a popover (Enter or Space opens it, focus goes to the first input, Escape closes it and returns focus to the button); from `xl` up it is inline in the bar.

**Items that do not fit the bar move into "More".** From `lg` up the top nav is priority-plus: the top-level items of your `nav` slot stay in the bar while they fit, and the rest move, in order, into a trailing "More" dropdown, so a bar with long labels or counts never overflows or runs under the actions. A group that moves shows as a labelled section inside "More"; when the current item has moved, "More" carries the current underline. Nothing to configure and no fixed widths: it is measured in the browser. "More" holds a copy of each moved item (the original stays in the bar, hidden), so a host that selects nav links in JS should scope its selector to `[data-atom-header-nav] > [data-atom-navlist]`, or skip `[data-atom-more-copy]`. In the bar, Escape closes the innermost thing that is open (a dropdown, the search popover, "More", then the menu panel) and nothing else. A `profile` slot with no `dropdown` is plain content in both shells, not a button.

**The `dropdown` slot is the account menu: pass menu items only.** Both shells wrap the slot in `<atom:menu>` themselves and print a default profile card (avatar or initials, then the name and email of `auth()->user()`), so put `<atom:menu.item>` rows and separators in `<x-slot:dropdown>` and nothing around them. A menu of your own around them double-wraps it (a second panel inside the first). Where the card sits depends on the shell: in the sidebar from `lg` up it is the trigger row at the foot of the side surface (the menu behind it holds your items only); in the sidebar's top bar below `lg`, and in the top nav, it is the head of the menu, above a separator and your items. The optional `profile` slot replaces the card in whichever place it sits. The avatar is read from `$user->avatar`: a URL is drawn as the image, and a user with no such attribute (or no value) gets initials. A model that throws on an attribute it does not have (`Model::preventAccessingMissingAttributes()`) counts as having none (only that exception: an accessor that throws something else still surfaces). The same goes for `name` and `email`. The initials come from `$user->initials()` when your User model has a public method of that name that returns a non-blank string. A model without one is not an error, and neither is an `initials()` that is protected (an Eloquent `Attribute` accessor) or returns something else (or a blank string): they are the first letters of the first two words of `$user->name`, uppercase (`tan joon long` gives `TJ`). Define `initials()` on the model to change that.

**Choosing the shell is yours.** Atom does not switch on an item count: it renders the nav before it knows where the nav goes. Decide in your own app layout, for example `<x-dynamic-component :component="$user->modules()->count() <= 2 ? 'atom::layouts.header' : 'atom::layouts.sidebar'">`. The choice is per page load; a role does not change mid-session, so `wire:navigate` never crosses shells.

## Page components

Replace copied `partials/section`, `breadcrumbs` and `heading` shells with `<atom:page>`. This is recommended, not required.

New, and additive: `<atom:page>`, `<atom:page.header>`, `<atom:page.tabs>`, `<atom:status>`, `<atom:stat>` and `<atom:stat.group>`. Start a screen with `<atom:page>` (it pads the page; the shell's `<main>` still does not) and `<atom:page.header>`, which holds the crumbs, the title, a status beside it, key facts, the actions and the one primary action for the record's current state. A level-1 `page.header` replaces the layout's hidden fallback `<h1>`, so a page that uses it has one `<h1>`. If your own partial shows crumbs in the header too, drop one of the two: the shell's `crumbs` slot or the header's `crumbs` prop.

**Which crumbs to use.** The header's `crumbs` prop is static: a list you pass in, drawn as links (the last is the current page), remembering nothing. The other kind is `<atom:breadcrumbs />` inside `@persist('breadcrumbs')`, a trail built from the pages the user actually came through, and the only thing that draws the Livewire component's `breadcrumbs()` method. That trail also keeps the URL the user left each page with (its filters and `?page=`), so a crumb or Back returns to the filtered listing, where the static prop goes to the `href` you wrote. A page that uses the header's `crumbs` instead of the persisted trail loses that "where you came from" trail, and its `breadcrumbs()` method feeds nothing there (`$_breadcrumbs` is still filled; nothing draws it). Choose the persisted trail for a listing people filter and come back to, and for a record reached from more than one place. Choose the header's `crumbs` for a fixed hierarchy (Settings, Billing) or a page with no Livewire component (the trail reads the page's component, so without one it has nothing to show). One on a page, never both.

**`<atom:empty>` is reworked.** It takes `kind` (`first-use`, `no-results`, the default, or `error`) and an `action` slot for the next step. Nothing you pass today stops working: with no `kind` it still reads "No Results" with the inbox icon, and `icon`, `heading`, `subheading`, `size`, `subtle` and the default slot behave as before. What changes is the look (design tokens in place of the zinc palette, a 16px heading, centred text) and the large state's icon, which draws itself in once, in about a second; not at `size="sm"` or `subtle`, and not under `prefers-reduced-motion`. A class or test that matched `py-8` or `bg-zinc-100` on it needs updating.

**Status.** A `badge` stays as it was. Use `<atom:status>` where a state is a word with a dot (`tone`: `neutral`, `info`, `success`, `warning`, `danger`, `accent`; `running` pulses the dot): it reads the role tokens, so it follows the theme and the accent without a `dark:` pair.

## Lists, forms and the timeline

Recommended: frameless tables inside panels; `numeric` on money columns; `form.section` for settings and forms. Everything here is additive (new props and components) except the look changes listed last.

**New.** `<atom:table frameless>` (no outer box, for a table inside a panel your page draws, under `<atom:table.views>` and `<atom:table.toolbar>`), `numeric` on `table.column` and `table.cell`, `<atom:table.toolbar>`, `<atom:table.views>` with `<atom:table.views.item>`, `<atom:form.section>`, a `bar` prop on `<atom:form.actions>` (the page save bar) and `<atom:timeline>` with `<atom:timeline.item>`. Read the four pattern pages (`resources/views/docs/patterns/list-page.blade.php`, `document-page`, `form-page`, `slide-over-form`) and copy their structure. Their data is invented.

**Saved views.** Bind `<atom:table.views wire:model.live="filters.view">` and pass `:counts` as an array keyed by the item's `value`, or a closure that gets the `value` and returns the count: atom never runs a count query, and a closure runs once per item on every render. With `WithUrlFilters`, declare `'view' => 'all'` in `urlFilters()` and have `isUrlFilterValid()` know the values; do not lock the property. Pass `:current` as well so the first paint is right.

**A view change clears a bulk selection, and does not reset the page.** `table.views` tells the table, as a filter does, so ticked rows are dropped when the view changes. Reset the page where you reset it for any filter (`updatedFilters()` calling `$this->resetPage()`); atom's filters do not do it for you.

**An item in a `@foreach` needs a `wire:key`** in `<atom:timeline>` (its folded state is Alpine's), and a `DateTimeInterface` `time` shows in the viewer's timezone.

**The toolbar belongs in the table's `header` slot, and counts do not.** In `<x-slot:header>` the table draws the strip around the toolbar and swaps it for the bulk-action bar while rows are ticked. The slot is an Alpine template, so text the server renders into it is not refreshed by a re-render (this was always so for the `header` slot): put a count or a total below the table. A toolbar placed elsewhere takes `strip`.

**The save bar is full width, its content on the page column.** `<atom:form.actions bar>` is sticky at the bottom of the screen. Its blurred surface and hairline run the full width of the screen's content area: from the sidebar's edge to the screen's right edge in the sidebar shell, from edge to edge in the top-nav shell. Its start slot and buttons stay aligned to the page column, whatever the page's `width`. The box itself is the page's padding box, padded by two CSS variables `<atom:page>` now sets (`--atom-gutter-s`, `--atom-gutter-e`; the page's bottom room is `--atom-page-pb`); the surface and the hairline are `::before` and `::after` that fill that box, and atom.css carries them on to the screen edge with a `box-shadow` spread clipped by a `clip-path` (the shells' `<main>` sets `--atom-bar-left` and `--atom-bar-right` for it). A shadow takes no layout room, so nothing scrolls sideways because of it, the page is never clipped, and it does not print smaller. The sidebar shell's bleed assumes the page starts at `<main>`'s content edge: do not put `mx-auto` on `<atom:page>` there. Outside a shell and an `<atom:page>` the variables are missing: the surface is the bar's own box, with a 16px gutter and no bottom room. In a modal or a slide-over keep using `sticky` (what `form.modal` draws).

**What changes in a table you already have.** No prop is removed, but the look moves with the tokens. A header cell is a 12px label at weight 510 in the muted tone, no longer uppercase or letter-spaced; its padding matches the cell's, so the two line up. A cell is on the row tokens: `px-row py-row leading-row` (9px by 12px, a 13px line at 1.35 in compact density) with a floor height (`--atom-row-min-h`: 40px compact, 44 comfortable, 48 on a touch device), not the old `py-3 px-4`. A table that sat in rows of 20px text is therefore a few pixels shorter, and one with a two-line cell is no longer squeezed. The header row no longer has the grey fill (`bg-zinc-100`, transparent in dark): it is on the panel's surface, with a hairline under it. A sortable header's dotted underline sits at `underline-offset-4` (it was 5) and takes the foreground colour. The row-actions cell (`table.actions`) is on the row tokens too, not `py-3 px-4`. The framed card is `rounded-panel border bg-surface` with no shadow, and the row hover is a faint fill. The table root is `min-w-0 max-w-full`, so a wide table inside a grid or flex parent (`<atom:page>` is one) scrolls inside its own box instead of widening the page: remove a `min-w-0` or `overflow-x-auto` wrapper you added to work around that. A test or a style that matched `uppercase`, `py-3`, `bg-zinc-100` or `rounded-lg` on a table needs updating.

**`form.actions sticky`** (the modal footer) now sits on the `surface` token (it was `bg-white dark:bg-zinc-900`, which are the same colours), is `z-10` (it was `z-1`), and its top border uses the border token (it was `border-zinc-100 dark:border-zinc-700`, a lighter line in light mode).

## Controls

Components use atom's tokens; remove overrides that patched the old look. Tag APIs are unchanged except where this section says so.

**`type="submit"` and `type="delete"` no longer add an icon by themselves** (a check and a trash can used to appear). Pass `icon="check"` or `icon="delete"` where you want one; a button with a label and no `icon` is a label.

**Button variants.** The tiers are `primary` (the solid accent, one per view), the default (bordered, no shadow) and `ghost`; `link` is text. Use `primary` for the accent button: `accent` still works, as an alias of it. `danger`, `warning`, `success` and `info` are solid tone fills, and `inverted` gives a tone's tint (`type="delete"` is the danger tint). `variant="outline"` and `"secondary"` were never defined and still draw the default tier, which is bordered: write the default (no `variant`). `facebook`, `google`, `linkedin`, `whatsapp` and `telegram` are the default tier with the brand's icon; they no longer paint the brand colour. `ghost` takes `color` (`accent`, `danger`, `warning`, `success`, `info`; `primary` still works as `accent`) and tints its text and its hover fill instead of filling solid on hover. Before this release `variant="primary"` was a near-black button that ignored your accent: if you relied on that, say `variant="primary"` and set `--color-accent`.

**Sizes come from the control token.** A button, input, select, date picker, time picker and tel field are 28px high in the compact density, 32 in comfortable and 40 on a touch device. A button's `sm` and `xs` sit 4px and 8px under that and `md` and `lg` 4px and 8px over (they were 32, 24, 48 and 56). Hosts that wrote `size="sm"` for a normal button next to a field should drop it: the default is the field's height. The text is 13px (14 in comfortable) and the icons 16px, where they were 20px. The OTP box is 40px square.

**Fields.** One recipe for every text control: a control border on the surface (a new role, `control-border`, which the field, the default button, the select and date triggers and the dropzone use: the hairline in light, a solid grey in dark), a 7px radius, no shadow, `fg-subtle` placeholder, sunken when disabled, the danger tone when `invalid` or in error. The ring is atom's `:focus-visible` outline, 2px, hugging the field's border (which takes the focus colour), where it was a 1px `focus:` outline in a grey. A select's option rows are 28px rows, and the calendar's selected day is `accent-ink` (it holds 3:1 on the panel in dark whatever your accent).

**Checkbox, radio, toggle.** A checkbox is 16px (was 18) with a 4px radius and a radio is now a 16px circle (it was a rounded square). A toggle is 36 by 20. Unchecked ones are stroked `fg-subtle`, checked ones are `accent-ink` with the mark in the surface colour, and none has a shadow.

**Slider, rating and uploader.** The slider's fill and thumb are `accent-ink` (they were near-black), its track 4px, with no thumb shadow; the rating's filled stars are `accent-ink` (they were amber `#f59e0b`) at 20px (they were 24px). A host that wants amber stars sets `color` on `[data-atom-rating-fill]`. The dropzone is a dashed hairline at radius 7.

**Calendar day cells are 36px.** A day in the date picker's calendar is 36px square (it was 40px), with 13px numbers (14 in the comfortable density), and the month label is that size at weight 590 (it was 20px). A host that sized the panel around the old cells needs to look at it.

**`button.group` lost `overflow-hidden` and its shadow.** The joined buttons share one hairline and keep the radius only at the group's two ends, so a focused button's ring is no longer cut off (a focused one is raised); the group draws no shadow of its own. A host that relied on the group clipping its contents, or on its shadow, has to supply that.

**New utilities.** `h-control-xs|sm|md|lg` and `min-h-control` follow the control token like `h-control`.

**Breaks a test.** A test or a style that matched `bg-primary`, `bg-white`, `shadow-xs`, `h-10`, `rounded-lg`, `size-4.5` or the focus classes on these components needs updating; the primary button is `bg-accent`.

**Dark check.** The dark-mode contrast check covers the 3a components and variants the two dark-mode hosts render: button (primary, default, ghost with a colour, danger, link, delete, submit, the Google and Facebook variants, sizes xs, sm and default, icon-only), input (text, email with chips, number, password, tel, colour, file through the uploader, OTP, icon, prefix and suffix), select (native, listbox single, multiple and searchable, filter), date picker (date, range, with time), textarea (autoresize, read-only), checkbox (caption, align start, card group), radio (group, card), toggle and uploader. Each text role is measured against its own surface at 4.5:1, and the boundaries and rings that identify a control at 3:1, in dark and light and for three accents (`tests/e2e/controls-*.spec.js`). Not measured, and left token-driven: the slider and the rating (no host uses them) and the standalone time picker (reached through the date picker's time row). The box of a control holds 3:1 in dark: `--atom-control-border` is `#726e65` there (3.6:1 on the surface it sits on, measured on a text field, a select, a date trigger and the default button; 3.1 on a raised panel), so the box reads clearly. In light it stays the design's 17% hairline, 1.41:1: an accepted exception to the 3:1 of WCAG 1.4.11, recorded in the spec report. `border-strong` is unchanged (panels, chips, dividers); utilities `border-control-border` and `bg-control-border` exist. Checkbox, radio and toggle, which have nothing else to identify them, hold 3:1 in both themes.

## Data display

Components use atom's tokens; remove overrides that patched the old look. Tag APIs are unchanged: no prop was added or removed. This batch is the table (every part), `badge`, `card`, `callout`, `dd`, `list`, `avatar`, `pagination`, `progress`, `skeleton` and `kbd`, and the small ones that go with them (`profile`, `placeholder-bar`, `navlist.badge`).

**Badge colours: `amber`, `indigo` and the rest map to tones.** A badge is now a borderless pill on the tone tokens (the tint with its own ink, 4.5:1 or more in light and dark), the same recipe as `<atom:status>` without the dot. `color` takes a tone, `black`, a hex, or any colour name a host might already pass, and a name that was never defined (`amber`, `indigo`, `teal`...) used to fall back to grey without saying so. The mapping:

| `color` | Reads as |
| --- | --- |
| `neutral`, `gray`, `grey`, `zinc`, `slate`, `stone`, `white`, none, and any name not listed here | neutral |
| `info`, `blue`, `sky`, `cyan`, `indigo` | info |
| `success`, `green`, `emerald`, `teal`, `lime` | success |
| `warning`, `yellow`, `amber`, `orange` | warning |
| `danger`, `error`, `red`, `rose` | danger |
| `accent`, `primary`, `purple`, `violet`, `fuchsia`, `pink` | accent (the colour of your brand, so purple is whatever your `--color-accent` is) |
| `black` | a solid pill: the foreground colour with the surface colour as text |

A name is read in lower case with its spaces trimmed. Two colours of one tone look the same (`blue` and `sky` are both info), so a screen that told them apart by hue now needs a word, an icon or a different tone. The check is `Jiannius\Atom\Services\Color::tone($name)`.

**A hex badge** (`#rgb`, `#rgba`, `#rrggbb`, `#rrggbbaa`, strict as before) keeps working, and its look is now derived: the tint is the hex at 14% over the surface, and the ink is the hex with its lightness held inside the accent-ink band (dark enough in light, light enough in dark), so every hex holds 4.5:1 on its own tint (all 216 colours of the web-safe cube were measured). A translucent hex is read as its opaque colour. Browsers below the relative-colour floor (Chrome and Edge 119, Safari 16.4, Firefox 128) get the foreground colour as the ink. The hex badge draws no border any more, and the `style` it renders has a `color`, a second `color` and a `background-color` (no `border-color`).

**Badge shape.** The sizes are 18px (`xs`), 22px (the default and `sm`) and 26px (`lg`) high, with 12px text (13px in `lg`); they used to be set by their padding, a border and a 6 to 8px radius. The group's `+N` is a 12px muted label. `data-tone` names the tone a named badge reads. The label is still text, never markup (pass an `HtmlString` for markup).

**`navlist.badge`** reads the same names (the count beside a nav item, and a menu item's `badgeColor`), so `amber` is warning there too; it kept its size and its tabular figures.

**Card.** A fill and a hairline at the 10px panel radius (it was 8px), with no shadow (it had `shadow-xs`) and 16px of padding (it was 24px): the cover bleeds by 16px, a divided card pads each child by 16px. `subtle` is the sunken fill with the hairline (it had no border). The stats variant draws its figure at 22px, weight 590 and tabular (it was 30px bold), its indicator in the success or danger ink with a 16px arrow (it was green and red 500 with a 20px one), and it no longer draws an outline on hover, only the sunken fill. The trend chart still takes its colour from the chart (a later batch).

**Callout.** The tint, a hairline of the tone's ink at 20%, the icon and the heading in the ink, and the text in the foreground, at the panel radius with 16 by 12px of padding (it was 24 by 16). The icon is 16px (it was 20), the dismiss button is a 28px target (it was a 28px box with 4px of padding and a 20px icon) and the text keeps clear of it. A heading you pass in the `heading` slot is wrapped in a `div` that carries the tone's ink, as before not bold. A link inside a callout is still `<atom:link>`, whose colour is a later batch's: on a warning or info tint it is below 4.5:1 in light until then.

**Description list.** The label is the `fg-muted` token (it was `muted` with a dark pair), the value is the foreground, and a long unbroken value wraps inside its box. The gap between the two columns is 12px.

**List.** The heading is a 12px muted label (it was 13px, uppercase); the rows have a 7px radius, tint on hover, have 6px of vertical padding (it was 8px) and 16px icons; the rail is the hairline token.

**Avatar.** The sizes are 24, 28, 34, 48 and 64px (`xs`, `sm`, the default, `lg`, `xl`; they were 26, 32, 40, 48 and 64), the squares have a 7px radius (10px from `lg`), and the fill is the neutral tone with a hairline instead of a grey with a shadow and a heavier border. Initials are weight 590. A stack rings each avatar in the surface colour so the overlap keeps its edge. `profile` has the name in the foreground and the email at 12px in the muted token.

**Progress.** The track is 6px high, 4px at `sm` (it was 10 and 6) on the strong hairline token. The bar is the accent ink by default (it was near-black in light and white in dark) and the tone's ink for `success`, `warning`, `danger` and `info` (the green, yellow, red and sky 500 are gone); the label is 12px, muted and tabular. The indeterminate bar stands still for a viewer who asked for reduced motion.

**Skeleton and placeholder bar** are the strong hairline token (it was `zinc-200`/`zinc-300`), pill-shaped, and the skeleton pulses only when the viewer has not asked for reduced motion (`motion-safe:animate-pulse`). The table's own loading skeleton does the same.

**Kbd.** A 20px key cap with a 6px radius, a strong hairline on the sunken fill and 12px muted text (it was a white cap with a zinc border).

**Pagination** (`<atom:pagination>` and the table's own pager). Every cell is as high as the control token (28px compact, 32 comfortable, 40 on a touch device; it was 32px) and at least as wide, with a 7px radius and tabular figures; the arrows are 16px (they were 20). The current page is on the neutral fill with a strong hairline and `aria-current="page"` (it was a dotted underline on grey). A disabled end is dimmed to half. The summary is 12px and muted, and the go-to-page box is a control (control border, surface, 7px radius). The `focus:ring` is gone: the keyboard ring is atom's `:focus-visible` outline, as on every control.

**The table's other parts.** The row checkbox is the 16px box of the checkbox control (it was 20px), empty with an `fg-subtle` edge or on the accent ink when ticked, with no dark pair. The bulk bar's count is muted and its links are the accent ink (they were `zinc-400` and the old `primary`). A filter chip is a 26px pill on the neutral tone with a muted name (it was a 14px-radius grey box), the overflow card of the filters is a panel (surface, hairline, 10px radius), the search's spinner and the loading spinner are muted tokens, and the archived toggle's pressed state is the foreground on the surface (the same look in both themes, without a `dark:` pair). Sorting, filters, selection, the checkbox column and the loading overlay are unchanged: these are class strings only.

**Breaks a test.** A test or a style that matched `bg-green-100`, `bg-sky-100`, `bg-zinc-100`, `rounded-lg`, `p-6`, `shadow-xs`, `size-8` (the pager), `size-10` (the avatar), `bg-green-500` (the progress bar), `animate-pulse` (now `motion-safe:animate-pulse`) or `bg-zinc-800! text-white!` (the archived toggle) on these components needs updating. A badge no longer has a `border` class or a border colour.

**Dark check.** The dark-mode contrast check covers the 3b components and variants the two dark-mode hosts render: table (sortable and right-aligned columns, a checkbox column, rows that link, a first-load skeleton), badge (`xs`, `sm` and default; a status enum or array; the names green, zinc, yellow, black, amber, gray, red, blue, purple and orange; a hex from a record), badge group, card (inset, subtle, divided, stats, chart), `dd` and `dd.group` in two columns, callout (warning, danger, info and success, with a heading and with its own icon), list and list item, avatar (`xs`, round) and avatar group, profile (`lg`), skeleton and placeholder bar. Each text role is measured against its own surface at 4.5:1 (an icon or a fill that carries meaning at 3:1) for three accents, one of them light, and the list, record and document pattern pages are read as pages in both themes. `kbd`, `progress` and the standalone pager are not rendered by those hosts; they are held to the same numbers anyway.

## Not restyled yet in the alpha

So far the tokens, the shells, `<atom:page>` and its parts (`page.header`, `page.tabs`), `<atom:status>`, `<atom:stat>`, `<atom:empty>`, the navigation lists, every part of the table, the new table, form and timeline parts, the footer of `form.actions`, the controls (`button`, `input` of every type, `textarea`, `select`, `checkbox`, `radio`, `toggle`, `slider`, `rating`, `date-picker`, `time-picker`, `uploader`) and the data display (`badge`, `card`, `callout`, `dd`, `list`, `avatar`, `profile`, `pagination`, `progress`, `skeleton`, `placeholder-bar`, `kbd`, `navlist.badge`) are restyled. The other components still carry their v3 `zinc`, `white`, `primary` and named-colour classes (the `zinc` ramp is atom's new warm neutral palette now, so they are not broken, only not in the new design yet), and are restyled in a later release. At the time of this batch that was: `alert`, `toast`, `modal`, `confirm`, `menu` (the shells' account menu is an `<atom:menu>`, and the panel of a select, a date picker and a dropdown is one too), `tabs`, `accordion`, `breadcrumbs`, `label`, `sharer`, `separator`, `copy`, `error` (a fixed red), `embed` (the old `muted` pair), `whatsapp` (its green is the brand colour, so it may stay), the editors, `command`, `tooltip`, `lightbox`, `calendar` and the typography components (`heading`, `caption`, `link`). Expect them to look slightly out of place beside the new shells and controls until then, and do not restyle them by hand: a class you override now is one more thing to delete.

One thing to know today:

- **A link inside a callout** is `<atom:link>`, which keeps its old colour until the typography batch: on a warning tint it is 3.5:1 in light (8.6 in dark), and about the same on an info tint.
